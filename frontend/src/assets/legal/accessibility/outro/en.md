## How this statement was prepared
Self-assessment carried out by the website owner, with automated technical verification (WCAG 2.1 AA) run every time the site is published.

## Feedback
To report an accessibility barrier not listed on this page, or to request content in an alternative format, contact the website owner using the details at the bottom of this page. If you do not receive a reply within a reasonable time, or the reply does not satisfy you, you can contact the [Agency for Digital Italy (AgID)](https://accessibilita.agid.gov.it), the supervisory authority for digital accessibility.
